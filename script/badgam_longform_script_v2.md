# "पचास गज" : Major Somnath Sharma, Badgam 1947
## Long-form Hindi script v2 (fact-checked, for recording)

Voice: Sarvam Bulbul v3, speaker **anand**. One request per numbered line.

**How to read this sheet**
- **Pace**: the Bulbul `pace` value for the line.
- **Pause**: silence added in the edit after the line, in seconds.
- **Tag**:
  - **V**: verified.
  - **A**: attributed in the narration.
  - **D**: disputed, with the range said aloud.

**Sources**
- **CIT**: the official PVC citation, as quoted on Wikipedia with Chakravorty 1995 as its source.
- **CLAWS**: Sameer Mallya, "The First Param Vir", *Scholar Warrior*, Spring 2015. It cites Cardozo's *Param Vir* (2003), Jasbir Singh's *Combat Diary* of 4 Kumaon (2010) and L.P. Sen's *Slender Was the Thread* (1969).
- **WIKI**: Wikipedia's articles on Somnath Sharma, the Battle of Badgam and the Param Vir Chakra, read in full this session.
- **TRIB**: The Tribune, 17 Nov 2023.
