## 1. YouTube long-form: ranking signals, benchmarks, packaging

### Takeaway
YouTube officially ranks on "valued watch time": clicks, watch time, post-view satisfaction surveys, shares, likes and dislikes together. No single metric decides. CTR only helps when retention holds up. Third-party benchmarks: CTR is typically 2-10% (half of all channels), with 4-10% "good". Average percentage viewed is about 24% platform-wide. YouTube's own intro benchmark is 50% or more still watching at 30 seconds. Test & Compare picks winners by watch time share, not CTR.

### Cited Findings
- **Official signals:** YouTube measures "valued watchtime" and uses post-view surveys to check satisfaction. Clicks, watch time, surveys, shares, likes and dislikes all "build on each other" to inform recommendations. — [YouTube Blog: On YouTube's recommendation system](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/)
- **CTR vs satisfaction:** "While CTR and watch time remain important, YouTube's system also accounts for user satisfaction gleaned through direct surveys and other feedback signals." — [Search Engine Journal, 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-youtubes-recommendation-system-works-in-2025/538379/)
- **Disputed claim:** Blogs claim "satisfaction has replaced watch time" or cite returning-viewer thresholds such as 15%. These are unverified third-party heuristics, not YouTube policy. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-viewer-satisfaction-algorithm-2026/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-youtubes-recommendation-system-works-in-2025/538379/)
- **Per-viewer assembly:** A secondhand report of a Sept 2026 Creator Insider conversation says recommendations are assembled per viewer and that no single metric indicates value. I could not verify it against the video. — [growmyfame](https://growmyfame.com/youtube-algorithm-myths-that-waste-creators-time/)
- **Context signals:** A 2025 Creator Insider interview with Todd Beaupré and Rene Ritchie discussed context signals such as device type and time of day. — [Prodvigate analysis](https://prodvigate.com/blog/changes-to-youtube-algorithms-a-detailed-analysis/)
- **CTR benchmarks:** YouTube has said roughly half of channels have an impressions CTR between 2% and 10%. Most channels average 4-6%. — [YTGrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ctr); [Wildnet](https://www.wildnettechnologies.com/blogs/what-is-a-good-ctr-on-youtube); [ClickStudio](https://www.clickstudio.co/blog/youtube-ctr-benchmarks)
- **Small-channel CTR:** Small channels often show higher CTR (6-10% under 1k subs, or 5-8%) because their impressions go mostly to existing viewers. CTR typically falls as a video is shown to colder audiences. — [Hooksnap 2026](https://www.hooksnap.io/blog/youtube-average-ctr-by-niche-2026); [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-analytics-for-small-channels-which-ones-matter-and-what-they-mean/)
- **Clickbait penalty:** YouTube is less likely to recommend a video with high CTR but low average view duration. — [ClickStudio](https://www.clickstudio.co/blog/youtube-ctr-benchmarks)
- **Retention study (secondhand):** A 2025 Retention Rabbit study of 10,000+ videos found 23.7% average percentage viewed platform-wide, and only 16.8% of videos kept more than half their audience. I could not locate the original report. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/); [Ylytic 2026 benchmarks](https://www.ylytic.com/blog/blog-youtube-benchmarks-2026)
- **New-channel retention:** "35-50% retention is strong for a new channel." This is a vendor heuristic. Vendor guides also suggest roughly 40-50% APV for 5-10 minute videos, without a sourced study. — [YTGrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ctr); [InstantViews benchmarks](https://instantviews.net/youtube-benchmarks)
- **Single-channel example:** vidIQ cites one of its own channels at 30.3% APV across 16.5M long-form views. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/)
- **First 30 seconds (official):** YouTube Studio's "Intro" metric is the % still watching at 30 seconds. Videos with 50% or more can appear in the channel's "above typical intros" list. YouTube recommends comparing against your own videos of similar length. — [InfluencerNotes summarising YouTube Help](https://influencernotes.com/academy/youtube-audience-retention-first-30-seconds); [Creator Playbook](https://www.creator-playbook.com/articles/your-intro-sucks-fix-first-30-seconds)
- **First 30 seconds (third-party):** Targets are 50-70% at 30 seconds for videos over 5 minutes, with 70% as an "ambitious working goal, not an official threshold." — [rsinc](https://www.rsinc.com/what-is-the-30-second-rule-on-youtube.php); [virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/retention-graphs-how-to-read-youtube-analytics-2026)
- **Test & Compare (official):** Tests up to 3 thumbnail variants. "Winner" means one variant clearly outperformed on watch time share, with statistical significance. "Preferred" means it likely did better, but not decisively. — [YouTube Help 13861714](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714)
- **Test & Compare titles:** Title and title-plus-thumbnail testing were added in late 2025. Some guides list the labels as Winner, Performed Same and Inconclusive, which conflicts with the Help page's wording. If there's no clear winner, the first variant is reportedly kept. — [VidAnalyze 2026](https://vidanalyze.com/news/youtube-ab-testing-for-titles-thumbnails-2026-guide); [ThumbnailTest](https://thumbnailtest.com/guides/youtube-test-compare/)
- **Auto-dubbing and multi-language audio:** Expanded to all creators in 27 languages around 4 Feb 2026. — [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/02/05/youtube-ai-auto-dubbing-all-creators-27-languages-xcxwbn/)
- **Dubbing results:** Creators who added dubbed tracks got more than 25% of their watch time from non-primary-language viewers on average. One less reliable source says above 33%. Jamie Oliver's channel reportedly tripled its views in the pilot. — [WinBuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/02/05/youtube-ai-auto-dubbing-all-creators-27-languages-xcxwbn/); [InfluencerMarketingHub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-studio-ai-stack/)
- **Lip-sync dubbing:** Still a limited pilot covering English, Spanish, German, Portuguese and French. — [Slator](https://slator.com/youtube-launches-automated-lip-sync-to-select-pool-of-creators/)
- **India translated viewing:** YouTube India data shows 77% of Gen Z in India watched creators in dubbed or translated formats in 2025. — [Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/insight/youtube-reports-that-77-of-gen-z-in-india-watched-creators-in-translated-formats-10892375); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/youtube-says-76-of-gen-z-sought-event-context-as-77-watched-translated-content-in-2025-85510.htm); [Google India blog, 2025 trends](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/2025s-biggest-trends-on-youtube-that-gave-india-a-new-shared-vernacular/)
- **Hindi vs Hinglish metadata (practitioner opinion only):** Guides recommend Hinglish or bilingual titles with English keywords, plus some Devanagari in the description so the spoken language is clear. Viewers search in both scripts, e.g. "हिंदी में…" and "hindi me…". — [Hookinator](https://hookinator-ai.vercel.app/blog/youtube-seo-hindi-content-creators); [Fluxnote Hinglish guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/hinglish-content-creation-guide-2026)
- **Disputed script claim:** One guide claims Devanagari titles signal a "Hindi algorithm cluster" and that mixed metadata confuses the algorithm. No YouTube documentation backs this. — [Fluxnote India algorithm guide](https://fluxnote.io/guides/indian-youtube-algorithm-insights-2026)

### Inferences
- For a Hindi war-story video, judge success on views from impressions combined with APV, not CTR alone. Realistic early targets for an 8-15 min story are a 4-8% CTR on browse traffic, 50%+ still watching at 30s (YouTube's own bar), and an APV in the 35-50% range. For 10 minutes, that is roughly 3.5-5 minutes average view duration.
- Use Test & Compare on every upload: 3 thumbnails, and a title test once available on the channel. It optimises watch time share, which matches what the algorithm rewards.
- Bilingual metadata looks like the safe default: a Hinglish (Roman) title with key English terms such as "Param Vir Chakra", a Devanagari phrase in the title or description, and Hindi audio declared correctly. Add an English auto-dub or uploaded track as a cheap experiment, since dubbing is available to all creators from 2026.

### Gaps
- No sourced benchmark for the CTR or APV of 8-15 min Hindi storytelling or documentary videos specifically.
- No sourced data on the optimal length for story/history videos in 2025-26. Practitioner consensus (8-15 min) is unverified here.
- Chapters and end screens: I found no 2025-26 data on their ranking effect. Background knowledge: chapters need 3+ timestamps starting at 0:00, each 10s+ long; end screens can be used in the last 5-20s. Verify on YouTube Help.
- Tags: background knowledge says YouTube Help states tags play a minimal role in discovery, mainly for misspellings, and that only the first 3 hashtags show above the title. Not verified this session.
- Whether Hindi is among the 27 auto-dubbing languages, and whether dubbing a Hindi source into English is supported, is unconfirmed.
- Returning-viewer weighting: no official number exists.

## 2. YouTube Shorts: ranking, length, effect on long-form, monetisation

### Takeaway
Shorts are distributed on whether people choose to watch rather than swipe ("Viewed vs. swiped away"), then on retention, loops and engagement. Third-party guides call about 70%+ viewed strong and below 30% weak. Max length has been 3 minutes since 15 Oct 2024, but third-party guidance still finds 15-60s (often 20-45s) performs best. YouTube's stated position is to keep Shorts on the same channel when the audience is the same. Since 2022 the system considers a channel's long-form videos for its Shorts viewers. Shorts pay out of a pooled 45% revenue share based on engaged views.

### Cited Findings
- **Swipe metric:** "Viewed vs. swiped away" is the share of Shorts-feed impressions where a viewer stayed instead of scrolling past. It is effectively the hook score of the first seconds. — [1of10 Shorts analytics](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/); [Prepublish](https://prepublish.ai/blog/viewed-vs-swiped-away-youtube-shorts)
- **Swipe benchmarks:** About 70%+ viewed is strong, around 50% is average and below 30% is weak. YouTube publishes no official target, so these are third-party estimates. — [GrowCreator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/viewed-vs-swiped-away-youtube-shorts); [TryGoViral](https://trygoviral.com/youtube-shorts-not-getting-views)
- **View counting:** From 31 March 2025, Shorts "views" count every start or replay. "Engaged views", the old definition, are kept and used for YPP eligibility and revenue sharing. — [CreatorEssentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/shorts-engaged-views/); [CreatorEssentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/engaged-views/)
- **Max length:** Raised from 60 seconds to 3 minutes for uploads on or after 15 Oct 2024. Shorts must be square or vertical. — [Variety](https://variety.com/2024/digital/news/youtube-shorts-maximum-video-length-three-minutes-1236166349/); [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators/)
- **Best length (third-party):** Klap puts the sweet spot at 15-60s, with completion falling after 1 minute. Turrboo says 20-45s is strongest. TubeBuddy argues length alone doesn't determine performance. — [Klap](https://klap.app/blog/max-length-of-youtube-shorts); [Turrboo](https://turrboo.com/blog/how-long-can-a-youtube-shorts-be); [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/three-minute-youtube-shorts/); [OpusClip retention](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention)
- **Same channel or separate:** Todd Beaupré (YouTube Director of Discovery) put it as "Same channel. Different channel": same audience means same channel. — [Panda Video](https://www.pandavideo.com/blog/shorts-and-long-form-videos-same-channel); [YTGrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/shorts-vs-long-form)
- **Shorts-to-long-form recommendations:** Since 2022 YouTube considers a channel's recent long-form videos when recommending to people who watch its Shorts. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-recommends-long-form-videos-shorts-viewers/)
- **Cannibalisation:** YouTube's position is that Shorts don't cannibalise long-form, because Shorts viewing is passive swiping while long-form is chosen deliberately. — [Everpop](https://everpop.app/blog/do-shorts-hurt-long-form-views); [Tubefilter, Rene Ritchie Shorts FAQ, Aug 2024](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/08/26/rene-ritchie-shorts-creator-faqs/)
- **Shorts monetisation:** YouTube pools Shorts-feed ad revenue, removes music licensing costs, and allocates by each creator's share of engaged views. Creators keep 45% of their allocation. — [PW Earners](https://www.pw.live/earners/exams/youtube-shorts-monetization-india-requirements-earnings); [StreamTVInsider](https://www.streamtvinsider.com/video/youtube-opens-revenue-floodgates-shorts)
- **YPP thresholds:** Full YPP requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months or 10M public Shorts views in 90 days. — [Daily Star](https://online.thedailystar.net/node/3219366); [Fluxnote 2026](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-monetization-requirements-2026-guide-2026)
- **India Shorts RPM:** Estimated at ₹5-40 per 1,000 views depending on niche. This is a blog estimate. — [CapsAI](https://capsai.co/blog/how-to-monetize-youtube-shorts-tips-for-indian-creators)

### Inferences
- Cut most story clips to 30-60s. Use the 60s-3min range only for a complete mini-story that holds attention, such as a full act of valour, and watch its viewed-vs-swiped figure closely.
- Keep Shorts on the main channel. The Shorts audience for Hindi war stories is the same audience as the long-form one.
- Shorts mainly build reach and subscribers. Revenue from them is small (₹ per 1,000 engaged views); the long-form video carries the RPM.

### Gaps
- **"Related video" link:** Background knowledge says that since late 2023 a Short can link to any of the channel's own public videos via the "Related video" field in Studio or the mobile upload flow. I could not source this or any click-through data in this session.
- **Lower YPP tier:** Background knowledge says the expanded tier (500 subs, 3 public uploads in 90 days, plus 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views in 90 days) has been available in India since 2023. Not verified this session, and search results did not confirm the 500 figure.
- I found no official data on the weight of loops versus completion.
- I found no quantitative 2025-26 study of Shorts' effect on long-form subscriber conversion.

## 3. Instagram Reels: ranking signals, originality, specs, timing

### Takeaway
Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes and sends as the top three signals. Creators should track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Sends per reach matters most for reaching non-followers. Reposted or watermarked content is kept out of recommendations to non-followers. Reels can be up to 20 minutes, but only those of 3 minutes or less are recommended to new audiences. Trial reels test with non-followers first. Hashtags are capped at 5 and don't drive reach.

### Cited Findings
- **Top signals:** On 22 Jan 2025 Mosseri named watch time, likes and sends as the most important signals. He told creators to track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Sends per reach is most important for non-follower reach, and likes weigh slightly more for followers. — [Zoomsphere](https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-what-actually-gets-reach); [BlckAlpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/instagram-algorithm-2026); [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)
- **Signal weights (disputed):** The "sends weigh 3-5x likes" claim is unsupported; Instagram has never published weights. — [SocialSync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/adam-mosseri-shares-instagram-algorithm-2026); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works)
- **Ranking principles:** Mosseri's Sept 2025 ranking videos reportedly named originality as one of four principles. — [Younus Fardeen](https://younusfardeen.com/blog/instagram-ranking-principles-mosseri-content-plan)
- **Originality and reposts:** Accounts that mainly post content they didn't create will not be recommended to non-followers. Followers still see their posts. Mosseri recommends remixing, or using repost or collab posts so the original creator gets credit. Coverage dated 30 Apr 2026, though sources conflict on the year; Instagram's originality push dates back to April 2022. — [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Disrupt Marketing](https://disruptmarketing.co/blog/instagram-original-content-update/); [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2303663)
- **Repost threshold (unverified):** The "10+ reposts in 30 days" figure circulates in blogs without a primary source. — [Disrupt Marketing](https://disruptmarketing.co/blog/instagram-original-content-update/)
- **Watermarks:** Reels with another app's logo or watermark are kept out of recommendations but still reach followers. One source attributes this to Mosseri, Oct 2024. — [FrameOS](https://frameos.studio/blog/how-to-cross-post-without-watermarks); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-watermarks-guide)
- **Length:** Reels went from 90 seconds to 3 minutes in Jan 2025 (announced 17-20 Jan). Later in 2025 the upload limit rose to 20 minutes, but Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to new audiences. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-three-minute-reels-8636683); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-announces-3-minute-reels-and-vertical-grids/); [CreateSocial](https://createsocial.ai/blog/how-long-can-instagram-reels-be); [Exemplary](https://exemplary.ai/blog/instagram-reels-length-limit)
- **Trial reels:** These go to non-followers first. Blogs advise keeping them to 3 minutes or less; there is no separate official length rule. — [CreateSocial](https://createsocial.ai/blog/how-long-can-instagram-reels-be)
- **Hashtags:** Since Dec 2025 posts and Reels allow up to 5 hashtags. Mosseri says hashtags don't increase reach but help classify a post. — [Toast Social, Dec 2025](https://toastsocial.substack.com/p/social-media-platform-updates-december); [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- **Posting times for India (low-quality evidence):** SocialPilot's dataset gives Monday 7:30am, 3pm and 7:30pm IST. Blogs favour 6-9pm IST on weekdays, with 9-10:30am or 12-1pm as alternatives. No authoritative India study was found. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=553523); [BossWallah](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/best-instagram-reels-posting-time-in-india-boost-views-engagement/); [Wildnet](https://www.wildnettechnologies.com/blogs/what-is-the-best-time-to-post-reels-on-instagram)

### Inferences
- Optimise Reels for sends: emotional or "you must know this hero" moments that viewers forward on WhatsApp. Hold the first 1-3 seconds, and keep Reels under 3 minutes (ideally under 90s).
- Upload clean, unwatermarked masters natively to each platform. Never upload a YouTube download or a file carrying the editor's or app's watermark.
- Use trial reels to A/B test hooks for the same moment with non-followers before posting to the grid.
- Start posting in the 6-9pm IST evening window, then switch to your own Insights data after about 4 weeks.

### Gaps
- I found no primary Mosseri or Instagram text this session; the Instagram findings rely on secondary reporting of his posts.
- I found no data on whether burned-in captions or Instagram's auto-captions affect reach.
- I found no audio guidance specific to 2025-26 (trending vs original audio).

## 4. Clipping strategy: one long video into many clips

### Takeaway
No rigorous study exists. Practitioner rules of thumb: a 10-minute video yields 3-5 strong clips; ambitious workflows target 10-15 moments, or about 0.4 strong moments per source minute. Post at a steady cadence of 3-5 clips per week, or 1-2 per day, rather than all at once. Cross-post without watermarks. TikTok is banned in India, so the targets are YouTube Shorts plus Instagram Reels, and optionally Facebook Reels.

### Cited Findings
- **Clips per source length:** 10-min source gives 3-5 clips; 30-min gives 8-12; 60-min gives 15-25. A monotone source yields fewer. — [LensPOV](https://lenspov.com/articles/repurposing-workflow-guide)
- **Higher targets:** Aim for 10-15 timestamped moments from 10 minutes ("if you can't find 10, your long-form isn't structured for repurposing"). Another estimate is about 0.4 strong moments per source minute. — [TikTube](https://tiktube.ai/blog/long-form-to-shorts-workflow-2026); [Lumina Clippers](https://luminaclippers.com/blog/how-to-repurpose-long-form-video-into-clips)
- **Cadence:** Guides suggest 3-5 Shorts/Reels per week for solo creators, or 1-2 per day. Steady spacing is said to beat dumping 20 clips at once; that is an assertion, not a test. — [PostQuick](https://www.postquick.ai/blog/how-to-repurpose-long-form-content-into-reels-and-shorts); [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-at-scale); [ShortVids](https://shortvids.co/how-to-repurpose-long-form-content/)
- **Watermark-free cross-posting:** Remove platform watermarks when cross-posting, because Instagram suppresses other apps' logos. — [FrameOS](https://frameos.studio/blog/how-to-cross-post-without-watermarks)
- **Format rule:** A Short must be vertical or square, so 16:9 cuts must be reframed to 9:16 or 1:1. — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators/)

### Inferences
- **Plan for a 10-15 min Hindi war-story video:** cut 5-8 clips.
  - About 3-4 self-contained clips (30-60s), each a complete mini-arc: setup, peak moment, payoff line.
  - About 2-3 cliffhanger or teaser clips that stop at the turning point, pointing to the full video via the Shorts "Related video" link and a pinned comment.
  - One 60-90s "trailer" clip.
- **Which moments:** the strongest emotional beat (sacrifice, last words), a shocking fact or number, the citation reading, any confrontation or decision point, and a "did you know" historical hook. Open each clip on the most intense line, not the context.
- **Reformat:** reframe to 9:16 with a centre or subject crop. Put bold Hindi or Hinglish burned-in captions inside the safe zone, about the middle 60% of the frame. Most short-form viewing starts muted, so captions also carry the hook.
- **Sequencing:** post 1-2 teaser Shorts/Reels 1-3 days before the premiere, the trailer on launch day linked to the long video, then 1 clip per day, or every other day, for 1-2 weeks. Steady spacing also keeps feeding returning viewers to the long-form video.
- **Cross-posting:** upload the same clean master natively to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, with platform-specific captions and hashtags. Optionally use Facebook Reels. Do not repost an Instagram export to YouTube or vice versa.

### Gaps
- No controlled 2025-26 study compares self-contained versus cliffhanger clips, or teaser-before versus clips-after sequencing; these are reasoned recommendations only.
- No data quantifying reach lift from burned-in captions.

## 5. Upload cadence and consistency for small channels

### Takeaway
Little hard 2025-26 data exists on long-form upload frequency. Official YouTube messaging (via Beaupré and Ritchie) stresses audience satisfaction over frequency. Practitioner consensus for a small channel is a sustainable long-form cadence of 1 every 1-2 weeks, backed by 3-7 Shorts per week cut from it.

### Cited Findings
- **Official framing:** Recommendations follow viewer satisfaction rather than any single metric, and there is no official upload-frequency rule. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/); [growmyfame (secondhand)](https://growmyfame.com/youtube-algorithm-myths-that-waste-creators-time/)
- **Short-form cadence:** Practitioner recommendations range from 3-5 per week to 1-2 per day. — [PostQuick](https://www.postquick.ai/blog/how-to-repurpose-long-form-content-into-reels-and-shorts); [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-at-scale)

### Inferences
- Researched Hindi war-story videos take a long time to make, so 1 long-form video every 2 weeks plus 5-8 clips per video (about 3-4 Shorts per week) is a sustainable cadence that keeps the channel active daily or near-daily.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2025-26 dataset (vidIQ, TubeBuddy, Social Blade, Buffer, Metricool) linking long-form upload frequency to small-channel growth in this session. Buffer and Metricool studies exist but could not be retrieved.
- I found no India-specific cadence or retention data.
